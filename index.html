How the instant quote works

  1. We run your model through a real slicing engine at its current size and orientation — the same kind of engine desktop slicers use, not a bounding-box guess.
  2. Filament cost is $0.60 per gram, and the weight comes straight from that slice's actual filament length × PLA density.
  3. Multicolor and large-format prints add a percentage surcharge for the extra handling.
  4. Every quote has a $20 minimum, and shipping/handling/tax are added at checkout.
